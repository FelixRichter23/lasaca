📐 The Complete Physics & Optical Budget (Step-by-Step)

1. TX Optics & Aperture Capture

Diode emits 51.0 W peak at 12.0°×25.0°. Lens (18 mm Ø) captures 89.5%. Transmitted: 43.4 W.

η_axis = 1 − exp(−2a²/w²) • P_tx = P_diode × η × T_AR

2. Beam Divergence & Target Spot

Collimated divergence: 2.00 × 3.28 mrad. At 200 m, spot size is 41.8 cm × 67.4 cm (Area: 0.221 m²).

θ_col = √( (w_emit / f)² + (4λ / π D)² ) • Spot = D_lens + 2 × Dist × tan(θ/2)

3. Atmospheric Attenuation

Visibility 23 km → γ = 0.163 /km. Transmission over 200 m: 96.8%.

γ = (3.912/V)·(λ/550nm)^(−q) • τ = exp(−γ·d)

4. RX Link Budget & Signal

Your 5 mm RX lens captures 0.0089% of spot energy. Pulse arrives as 3.85 mW.

P_rx = P_tx × (A_rx / A_spot) × τ_atm • I_sig = P_rx × 0.65 A/W

5. Solar Noise & AC-Filter

Direct sunlight injects 5.81 mW DC into the detector. Shot noise: 0.02 mV RMS (BW 175 MHz).

BW ≈ 0.35/t_rise • i_noise = √(2 × q × I_solar × B)

6. IEC 60825-1 Class 1 AEL

α = 2.0 mrad → C6 = 1.33; C4 = 2.57; C5 = 0.40. Single-pulse AEL: 26.4 nJ.

AEL_sp = 7.7×10⁻⁸ × C4×C6×C7 [J] • C5 = 5·N^(−0.25) ≥ 0.4
